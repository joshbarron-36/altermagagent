Which of your old calculation folders actually support the claims in your paper, and what should you run next?

At the Open Scientific Intelligence Hackathon we built AltermagAgent, a free and open tool that reads VASP folder trees without changing them.

It groups the runs into branches by structure, magnetic realization and spin-orbit coupling, draws them like a git history, and tags every finding: output found, parsed, validated or insufficient evidence.

It is an honest diagnosis, not a verdict. It names unconverged runs, missing steps and the next calculation, and it never calls a material an altermagnet from the files alone.

On two real projects, CrSe and MnTe, its computed magnetic space groups matched the physicist's own findsym results.

Built by Diana Golovanova (Weizmann Institute of Science), Poulami Sadhukhan (CSIR National Chemical Laboratory) and Josh Barron.
